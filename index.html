01 / ABOUT THE CLUB

You belong
in the room.

Our purpose is to enrich the computer science culture at Miramar College and other San Diego Community College District campuses. We bring students together to learn, create, and connect with the wider community.

Whether you’re writing your first program or building your next project, you can share ideas, find help with coursework, and learn alongside other students.

05 / GET INVOLVED

Let’s make
something happen.

Membership is open to interested Miramar College students, regardless of background or identity. The club constitution defines active membership for students enrolled in at least one Miramar unit.

Come to a meeting, bring an idea, or just listen in. Join the club Discord server to connect with other members. For club questions, contact faculty advisor Andrew Huang.
